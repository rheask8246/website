About

Machine learning researcher and computational astrophysicist building intelligent systems for scientific discovery.

Photo

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at UC San Diego, researching scientific machine learning, autonomous sensing systems, graph neural networks, and generative AI for simulation and scientific discovery.

Previously, I worked at MIT Lincoln Laboratory in the Space Systems Analysis and Test Group, where I developed signal processing pipelines, RF system interfaces, reinforcement learning systems for satellite coordination, and generative models for radar image classification.

At UC Berkeley Space Sciences Laboratory, I worked on machine learning reconstruction systems for gamma-ray astrophysics missions including COSI, GAPS, BTO, and AMEGO-X in collaboration with NASA teams.
